Career

Since I knew how to find small vulnerabilites in games and all, I wanted to find out if there were any jobs in cybersecurity that utilized my skill, which led me to find out about Penetration Testing. Penetration Testing (also shorted to Pen Testing), is defined as "A method of testing where testers target individual binary components or the application as a whole to determine whether intra or intercomponent vulnerabilities can be exploited to compromise the application, its data, or its environment resources." [https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/penetration_testing]

AI Usage

When it comes to penetration testing, AI has a major impact due to how quickly it can perform tasks such as reconnaissance, scanning, and analysis. It can be used to web crawl through different sites and find leaked data and broken infrastructure, allowing testers to easily report the data. It can also be used to simulate attacks using older methods, allowing testers to find new or unpatched vulnerabilities. Overall, it’s a major tool that helps with a task that can be time intensive.
